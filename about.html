About YAZI

Why YAZI exists

Searching online for how to apply or where to study can bring up too much information, and much of it doesn’t fit your situation. YAZI narrows it down. It gives simple, accessible and reliable information, so you know where to start and what to do next, including routes many people don’t know about, such as TVET colleges.

About the founder

Portia Zindlani

YAZI: From Insight to Impact

Portia Zindlani is a final-year Education student at the University of Pretoria and the founder of YAZI.

Portia is a member of the Make a Difference Leadership Foundation, which focuses on leadership development. She has developed a passion for mentorship, leadership and empowering young people. Through bursaries and scholarships, she has experienced firsthand how access to information can open doors and transform lives. This inspires her belief that everyone deserves access to reliable information and equal opportunities to reach their full potential.

Through YAZI, which means “to know something”, Portia is closing the information gap that holds young people back. She noticed that many learners do not fail because they lack ability, but because they do not know what opportunities exist, where to start, or where to find reliable information.

She has seen learners with good grades believe their options had come to an end, simply because they did not know about alternative routes like TVET colleges, or how to apply for university and funding.

YAZI exists to change that. It is a platform, starting as a website and potentially an app in the future, that turns what is wide and overwhelming into simple, accessible and reliable steps: whether it is applying for university, finding funding, or writing a Curriculum Vitae.

How we keep information accurate

Each option shows the date it was last verified and links to the official source. Information that has not been checked recently is marked so you know to confirm it. We review content regularly and before the main application seasons.

Privacy

YAZI does not ask you to create an account and does not collect personal information such as your name, ID number or contact details. The answers you give in the options finder are used in your browser to show your results and are not sent to us.

Our hosting provider may keep standard technical logs, such as IP addresses, to run and secure the site. If we add analytics or any feature that collects personal information, we will update this page first and handle that information in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA). YAZI is responsible for this website. Questions about privacy can be sent to us using the details under Contact us.

If you choose light or dark mode, that choice is saved in your browser on your device so the site remembers it. It contains no personal information.

If you use the WhatsApp share button, the message is created on your device and sent through WhatsApp under its own terms.
